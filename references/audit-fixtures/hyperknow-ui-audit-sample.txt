Study topic: Photosynthesis basics

Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy. In plants, the
light-dependent reactions occur in thylakoid membranes and produce ATP and
NADPH. The Calvin cycle occurs in the stroma and uses ATP and NADPH to help fix
carbon dioxide into carbohydrates.

Review prompts:
1. Where do the light-dependent reactions occur?
2. What two energy-carrying products support the Calvin cycle?
3. Where does the Calvin cycle occur?
